# Bloodhunt: Shadows of Lesvos — Design Concept Summary

## Genre
Dark Fantasy Action-Adventure with RPG, horror and stealth influences.

## Core Narrative
Professor Wolfmane lives a double life in Mytilene: academic by day and werewolf vigilante by night. The central themes are redemption, survival, justice, moral conflict and the tension between rational control and instinct.

## World and Structure
The broader concept places the player across a stylized Lesvos, with the University of the Aegean functioning as a safe/research space and areas such as the city, castle, port and forests acting as challenge spaces.

## Conceptual Core Loop
**Investigation → Preparation → Hunt → Consequences → Return**

The concept combines information gathering with action-oriented nighttime encounters.

## Broader Systems Explored in the Design Document

- Day/night gameplay structure.
- Bloodlust / Humanity management.
- Safe zones and challenge zones.
- Investigation and information gathering.
- Physical combat and stealth.
- NPC dialogue and reputation consequences.
- Morality-driven world-state changes and endings.
- Adaptive and interactive audio.
- Diegetic, non-diegetic, spatial and HUD feedback.
- Skill progression and flow/balance considerations.

## Prototype vs. Concept

The current playable Unity prototype implements a smaller, focused subset of this design: clue collection, weapon acquisition, objective progression, vampire encounters, hostage rescue, a mini-boss encounter and a final boss sequence.

The systems listed above represent the broader design vision and potential future development rather than a claim that every feature is implemented in the current build.
